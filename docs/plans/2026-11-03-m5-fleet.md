# Mission 5: the Mediterranean Fleet (phase E, §30, 32.7x)

Spec: `docs/designs/2026-10-13-land-turn-design.md`. Its *What "done"
means* leaves phases E and L skipped; with patrols (#110) phase L opens,
so phase E is the last phase every game still passes through. cna
restates the fleet in `rules/90-special.md` (*The Mediterranean Fleet*,
SPI 30.x) and the abstract block's strikes on it in
`rules/95-abstract-logistics-and-air.md` (*Bombardment of the fleet*,
32.7x). Every decision here is the autopilot's under Brian's delegation
of 2026-09-26 and is reversible with one PR.

**Goal.** The Commonwealth's warships take the field: placed each stage
off the coast, barraging coastal hexes, struck in Alexandria by the Axis,
cycling through port. Graziani's Offensive starts with eleven of them
(`sides.cw.fleet`), and the Bardia–Tobruk coast is where the Italians
stand, so the fleet is a real weapon in the first scenario two players
pick. Layforce (27.7x) needs a ship to land from, so it waits on this.
This serves **The goal**: every rule of the Land Game enforced.

**Non-goals.** The chariot raid (30.4x: campaign games only, after July
1941; a later slice). Naval transport of troops (30.5x: port capacities
are the Logistics Game's 55.3; under the abstract block only supply units
go by sea, 32.3x). Air bombing of ships (Air Game). The *San Giorgio*
until cna gives its values. Variant V-001.

**Blocked on cna.** Each ship's gun and AA ratings are on its counter, not
in `data/tables/cw-fleet-schedule.json`, which gives names, types and
arrivals only. Requested in `PROGRESS.md` (*Warship ratings for the
fleet*). Task 1 does not need them; Tasks 2 and 3 do.

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Module | a new `fleet` module (`#[serde(default)]`), in `Modules::ALL` once the browser has a panel, as patrols did | old saves and logs replay unchanged |
| Pieces | `PieceKind::Ship`, one per schedule row, Commonwealth; no stacking value, no ZOC, never blocks movement; arrives by the schedule in Alexandria; the Valletta options are a set-up choice | 30.1x, 30.59 |
| Where a ship may be | a sea or coastal hex reached by sea from Alexandria and no farther west than row xx29 on sheet B: R-019 makes the row govern (the engine counts four of its hexes at 101–103 sea hexes, so a 100-hex cap as well would refuse the row); Alexandria and Valletta are its ports | 30.1x, R-019 |
| Port cycle | each ship records stages at sea in a row and stages in port since; at most three at sea in a row, then two in port for each stage at sea; a ship that fired spends the next two stages in Alexandria. The Valletta passage is a stage neither at sea nor in port | 30.1x, 30.25 |
| Phase E | the Commonwealth places each ship it chooses (`Order::Station { ship, hex }`), then `Pass`; a ship not placed stays in port. Repair runs as the phase closes: one gun point per six consecutive stages in Alexandria | 20's sequence, 30.37 |
| Bombardment | a ship fires once a stage in a barrage step of its owner's choosing, its gun rating as artillery points needing no ammunition, into its own coastal hex, or an adjacent one at half for 4-point ships; through the existing barrage code | 30.2x |
| Strikes on the fleet | at set-up the Axis rolls a die (+2 in a campaign game) for its number of strikes, hidden from the Commonwealth; one a stage, plotted in phase E, resolved with no strike while sheet E has a storm; the column die and two dice on the Air Bombardment table give damage, which the engine spreads over ships in Alexandria biggest first | 32.7x; the Axis's choice is made by the engine, as patrols' defender's is |
| Damage | a ship's guns drop by its damage and its AA in proportion; below half its printed guns it may not leave port; at zero it is sunk | 30.3x |
| What is seen | ships and their stations are open (the counters are on the map); the Axis's strike count is its own until spent | 3.61 |
