# Mission 5: patrols (phase L, §16)

Spec: `docs/designs/2026-10-13-land-turn-design.md`, which skips phase L
on the grounds that "patrol points belong to the Air Game". They don't.
cna restates patrols as a land procedure (`rules/60-combat.md`, *Patrols
and reconnaissance*, SPI 16.x), with its three tables in `data/tables/`.
Under the abstract block a patrol pays nothing
(`rules/95-abstract-logistics-and-air.md`). Every decision here is the
autopilot's under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26 and is reversible with
one PR.

**Goal.** Phase L stops being skipped. Online play hides each side's units
(3.61), and a patrol is the land game's way to buy that information back.
Without patrols a networked player cannot learn what holds a hex short of
attacking it. This serves **The goal**: two players at a distance, every
rule enforced.

**Non-goals.** Dummy tank formations (16.4: no counters, a construction
spend; they stay in `NOT_SIMULATED.md`). Fuel and ammunition (none under
the abstract block). Air reconnaissance (Air Game).

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| When | Phase L opens for the phasing player when it fired no anti-armour and made no close assault this stage (16.21); otherwise it stays skipped | 16.2x; the sequence of play's L |
| Order | `Order::Patrol { sources: Vec<(PieceId, u32)>, target: HexId, exclude: Vec<PieceId> }`; `Pass` ends L | one patrol per order; no limit on the number (16.21) |
| Eligible points | recce (OA `vehicle`), light tanks (the weapons 16.11 and E-010 name), motorised and mechanised infantry TOE; at most 2 a hex, 3 a target; cohesion above −8; no rainstorm or sandstorm hex | 16.1x |
| Target | an enemy-occupied hex within 5 hexes by a path the units could pass, avoiding enemy-occupied and enemy-controlled hexes except the target's own ZOC; not barraged this stage | 16.22 |
| Rolls | three dice in order on the game's RNG: survival (skipped with no combat units in the target, −1 if all recce), reconnaissance on the surviving points, objective loss | 16.3x, 16.5–16.8 |
| Defender's choices | made by the engine: which units are described (battalions first, never HQ or AA while combat units are present, never an excluded unit, then by id); TOE stated exactly, not "within two"; the TOE lost by the losses order the combat code already uses | a pending answer per patrol would stall play for a choice that rarely matters; a `NOT_SIMULATED` row says so. A later slice can make it a sealed answer |
| Patroller's losses | killed points come off the patrolling units, captured ones also become the defender's prisoner points | 16.31, §28 points |
| What is seen | `Event::Patrolled` is public, its descriptions included: they are what the patrol bought. The view and the leak predicate let a described unit's stated TOE through in that event alone | 3.61 limits intelligence; a patrol is its sanctioned breach |
