# Mission 5, slices 3–6: task lists

Each slice's plan is expanded into its own file (`…-m5-slice-N-….md`, in
slices 1 and 2's shape) at the start of the slice, against the code as it
then stands. The spec is `docs/designs/2026-10-13-land-turn-design.md`.

## Slice 3: supply movement (phases C, G, H, J)

- Phase C stops being skipped: a reorganisation segment with orders to
  attach and detach motorisation points to supply units (30 real, 6
  dummy) and to units (32.5x: historically motorised battalions first),
  to rearrange loads among supply units in a hex (32.1x), and the
  once-a-turn air lift (32.3x).
- A supply unit with its points moves in G only starting and staying
  stacked with a friendly combat unit, or in H, not both (32.3x); CPA 15
  when carrying supply (32.5x); may react unless the approacher has CPA
  35+.
- Phase J: the Commonwealth's two supply units per stage, one direction,
  on the railway to D3714.
- Destroying one's own supply (32.1x), with its CP and die roll.
- Motorisation points captured with the unit they carry (32.5x).
- Panels: an organisation panel for phase C; supply moves in the move
  panel; a rail panel.

## Slice 4: arrivals (the naval convoy stage, phase D)

- The clock gains the turn's naval convoy stage (sequence II).
- Planning rolls on `simplified-supply.json` (32.4x): Axis two turns
  ahead under the planning month's letter, Commonwealth four ahead under
  the arrival period; none in a scenario's first turn.
- Axis convoys by lane, at most three supply units and twenty
  motorisation points per lane, bombed on `axis-convoy-bombing.json` and
  the Air Bombardment Table (32.6x).
- Commonwealth arrivals in Cairo, one in three at Alexandria.
- Monthly motorisation losses (32.5x, `motorisation-losses.json`).
- Dummies issued one per three real units (32.1x).

## Slice 5: breakdown and repair (segment G-breakdown, phase K)

- Breakdown points accumulated per stack and vehicle class while moving
  (terrain `breakdown` column, weather), the column shifts (class, `bar`,
  hot, sandstorm), two dice on `breakdown.json` (21.3x); broken-down TOE
  points to a marker in the hex; motorisation points exempt (32.5x).
- Towing (21.6x) and phase K field repair (§22, `vehicle-repair.json`).
- Markers in the stack panel; a repair panel.

## Slice 6: organisation, dumps, prisoners, docs

- §19 attach and detach in phase C with `max-attachments.json` and
  `formation-organisation.json`; battle groups (19.7x) and ad hoc
  anti-tank units (19.8x) if Graziani's OA allows them.
- 24.9 dumps in phase C's construction segment, costed by
  `cna-rules::construction::dump_cost` under R-018.
- §28 prisoners: guards, the phase-H march, `prisoners-captured.json`.
- Docs: README, `NOT_SIMULATED.md`, the spec's Status, the queue row.
